# Halyard — Series A deck (synthetic demo company)

## The problem
Over 80% of global trade by volume moves by sea, yet most port and freight operators still coordinate berths, crews and cargo over email and spreadsheets.

## The product
Halyard is the operating system for mid-sized shipping and port operators: berth scheduling, crew rosters, cargo manifests and customs paperwork in one workspace.

## Traction
- **40+ enterprise customers** across North America and Northern Europe
- **$4.2M ARR**, growing 9% month over month
- **131% net revenue retention**
- 76% gross margin

## Market
The global maritime software market is roughly **$12B** today.
